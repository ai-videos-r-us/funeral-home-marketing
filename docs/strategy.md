# Strategy: why this exists and how it brings in clients

**Goal:** make Next Chapter Media the name UK funeral directors know, starting in South West England, and turn that recognition into local SEO clients. Our main offer is local SEO for funeral directors.

## The assets
1. **The directory** (funeraldirectorawards.com): every South West funeral director, with details sourced from their own websites. It's for families, and it's what AI tools and Google cite. It also serves as live proof that we can rank a site.
2. **The awards:** independent and free to enter. They're what make the site credible to the trade, and they give us a reason to contact every firm.
3. **The newsletter:** the owned audience of funeral directors. This is the real moat.

## The funnel
1. **Listing live:** email (limited companies only), LinkedIn, or letter: "your listing is live, please check it's accurate." No pitch. They claim the listing for free and are invited to the newsletter.
2. **Local visibility snapshot:** from directory data, how a firm compares with others in its town (reviews, price list visibility, services its website doesn't mention). This opens the phone conversation.
3. **Nominations open:** firms share the link with families.
4. **Newsletter:** useful every issue, with one light services mention.
5. **After results:** award promotion packages for winners and finalists (award on their site, schema, press release, badge). That's local SEO work that comes straight out of the awards.

Best-fit buyers: independents with 2–10 branches. Corporate groups buy centrally and won't come through this funnel.

## Rules that protect the brand
See `editorial-policy.md`. The short version: nothing is for sale on the site, clients get no special treatment, ownership is disclosed, and no pitching during the shortlist window.

## Success measures (first region, 6 months after launch)
- At least 10% of listed firms have claimed or corrected their listing
- At least 300 newsletter subscribers
- At least 3 paying clients traceable to this funnel

If we're well short, keep the site as a credibility asset but stop spending effort expanding to new regions.

## Expansion
Only after the success measures are met. Next regions use the same blueprint: rerun the gates with a new region, then move to multi-region URLs (`/directory/<region>/<town>/`). Plan that change before the second region, not after.
