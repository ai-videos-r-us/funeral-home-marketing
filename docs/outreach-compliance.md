# Outreach and newsletter: compliance checklist

This is a practical checklist, not legal advice. Confirm anything uncertain with the ICO guidance or a solicitor before the first send.

## Before any outreach
- [ ] **ICO data protection fee** paid for AI Video Systems (required when processing personal data such as claims, nominations and newsletter sign-ups).
- [ ] **Privacy notice** on the site covering claims, nominations, the newsletter and outreach, including the lawful basis (legitimate interests for B2B outreach, consent for the newsletter).
- [ ] **Legitimate interests assessment** written and kept, for contacting funeral directors about their own listing and the awards.

## Email (PECR)
- **Limited companies and LLPs** ("corporate subscribers"): unsolicited marketing email is allowed if it identifies the sender, has a working unsubscribe, and you honour opt-outs.
- **Sole traders and partnerships** count as individuals: marketing email needs prior consent. Many independent funeral directors are sole traders or partnerships.
- **Before a firm enters any email sequence**, check its status at Companies House (company number, type). No company record usually means sole trader or partnership, so **no marketing email**: use LinkedIn, phone (see below) or post instead.
- A message saying "your listing is live, please check it's accurate" is primarily a service message, but if it also promotes services it counts as marketing. Keep the first touch free of any pitch.
- Send from secondary domains in the real business name (for example `getfuneraldirectorawards.com`) to protect the main domain's deliverability. **Never** use domains that resemble another organisation (NAFD, SAIF, Good Funeral Awards, competitors).
- Every email: sender identity (Next Chapter Media, trading name of AI Video Systems), postal address, unsubscribe link, and a suppression list shared across every sending domain.

## Phone (PECR, CTPS)
- Screen every number against the **CTPS** (Corporate Telephone Preference Service) and the **TPS** (sole traders can register on the TPS) before unsolicited sales calls. Don't make sales calls to registered numbers.
- Calls about the accuracy of a listing or an award nomination are not sales calls. If the conversation moves to services, let the funeral director lead it.
- Never pitch shortlisted firms during the awards firewall window.

## LinkedIn
Personal, one-to-one messages. No automated connection or messaging tools that break LinkedIn's terms.

## Newsletter
- Double opt-in sign-up (on the claim form, nomination confirmations and the site). Consent recorded with date and source.
- Content: a monthly South West funeral price summary from directory data, CMA and FCA rule changes, awards news, anonymised marketing case studies. **At most one Next Chapter Media slot per issue.**
- Once someone has subscribed with consent, they can receive marketing email regardless of company type.

## Data the build collects
DataForSEO rows can include web-sourced email addresses. Check each against the firm's own website domain, bounce-test before use, and record the source. Never buy email lists.
