# Monetization Slots: not used

The original blueprint sold featured slots, premium listings and best-of placements, and pinned paid positions on city pages. **None of that exists in Funeral Director Awards.** The template ignores the `featured`, `premium` and `best_of_rank` columns, and the featured page, featured band, paid badges and tier sheet have been removed.

What replaces it:
- **The claim flow stays, free.** Every listing page shows "Is this your business? Claim it." Each claim is verified by a person (email domain matches the website, or the phone matches the Google listing) before the CSV changes. A verified claim unlocks owner photos, hours and the map for any business.
- **Badges exist only for real award wins** ([[awards-programme]] in `docs/awards-programme.md`).
- **The commercial side** is Next Chapter Media's own services, offered to funeral directors under the rules in `docs/editorial-policy.md` and `docs/outreach-compliance.md`. Nothing on the site is sold.

See `docs/strategy.md` for how the directory supports client acquisition.
