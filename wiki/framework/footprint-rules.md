# Footprint Rules: replaced

The original rules hid the publisher's preference for its own sites (no Search Console, separate accounts, varied registrant details). **Funeral Director Awards does the opposite** (see `docs/editorial-policy.md`):

- Ownership is disclosed on About, the How we score page and the FAQ: Next Chapter Media, a trading name of AI Video Systems, a marketing agency that works with funeral directors.
- Google Search Console is used, verified in the real business name.
- Clients are listed like any other business: same card, same template, same link treatment (`site.outboundFollow`), same order. The `owned` flag from Gate 2 must never change rendering.
